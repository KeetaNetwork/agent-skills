Basics path

Wallet, KYC, pay in, then pay out.

A new account follows one order. Create the wallet, complete individual KYC, request US bank deposit instructions, confirm the Keeta USD balance, and pay out only when the user asks.

  1. 01 Create wallet

    Create or restore a signer and confirm test or main.

  2. 02 Complete KYC

    Use the Footprint provider when Resolver returns it.

  3. 03 Pay in

    Show the US bank deposit instructions the provider returns.

  4. 04 Check balance

    Read the Keeta USD balance and history after the bank payment.

  5. 05 Pay out

    Optional. Send Keeta USD to a confirmed US bank recipient.

Example prompts

  • Create a Keeta wallet on the test network and show me only the public address.
  • Complete individual KYC for this wallet and attach the certificate after I finish the provider page.
  • Get US bank deposit instructions that credit Keeta USD, then check the balance.
  • After the deposit is visible, pay out Keeta USD to this US bank account.
