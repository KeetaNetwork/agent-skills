Native multi-asset

One wallet. More than one money rail.

Keeta agents work with native multi-asset balances, on-chain service discovery, reusable identity certificates, and partner rails: named bank accounts and global payouts from Bivo, Visa Direct card payouts, Bridge.xyz bank transfers, and LayerZero bridging.

01 Native assets KTA · USD · EUR · token accounts
02 Discoverable anchors Bivo · Bridge.xyz · LayerZero · OneFootprint
03 External rails ~50 countries · Visa Direct · Base · Solana · x402

Partners

The partners behind every rail.

Agents find each provider through on-chain discovery, so there are no partner API keys to manage. Availability depends on the user’s country and account.

Banks

Bivo

A US bank account in the user’s own name, wire and SWIFT deposits, and bank payouts in about 50 countries.

Cards

Visa Direct

Pay out to, or top up from, the user’s own debit card through Bivo, where the app offers secure card entry.

Banks & chains

Bridge.xyz

USDC to and from US bank accounts, EURC to SEPA, and USDC on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Avalanche, and Polygon.

Chains

Keeta Base bridge

KTA, USDC, EURC, and cbBTC between Keeta and Base, one to one, with no KYC.

Chains

LayerZero

About 50 tokens on 10 EVM chains, plus withdrawals to Solana, routed to Keeta through Base.

Identity

OneFootprint

Verifies a person once. Providers accept the resulting Keeta certificate, and each sees only what it asks for.
